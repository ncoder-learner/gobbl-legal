Delete Your Account

Last updated: July 2026

This page explains how to delete your Gobbl account and what happens when you do.

1. How to Delete Your Account In-App

To delete your account:

  • Open the Gobbl app and sign in
  • Go to the Account screen
  • Tap Delete Account
  • Confirm the deletion when prompted

2. What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently delete the following data associated with it:

  • Your posts
  • Your logged meals
  • Your photos
  • Your comments
  • Your likes
  • Blocks you've placed on other users
  • Reports you've submitted
  • Your friendships and friend connections
  • Your account itself, including your email, profile information, and login credentials

3. This Action Is Permanent

Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once your account and data are deleted, we cannot recover them for you. Please be certain before confirming deletion.

4. Can't Access the App?

If you're unable to access the app but want your account and data deleted, email us at gobbldevofficial@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, and we'll process the deletion on your behalf.
